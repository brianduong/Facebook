Straight down there, second turn on the left, then past the big tree, and you'll see it.

To you, it's crystal clear. You've walked it a hundred times.

But they've never seen that tree. Which junction counts as the second? They nod, say thanks, and walk off still lost.

Sometimes all it takes is ten steps. Walk them to the corner and point: that yellow gate, right there.

They won't remember your face. But they'll remember that once, in a strange city, someone walked them all the way to the gate.
