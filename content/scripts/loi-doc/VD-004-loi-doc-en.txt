Your thank-you is being wasted. It's missing one word.

Thanks. It's the thing we say most, and the thing people forget fastest. Polite, but it slides right off.

The person hearing it doesn't learn anything. They just know you were being polite.

Add one word and it changes completely. The word is because.

Thanks becomes thank you for always leaving a plate of food out for me.

Thanks becomes thank you for not asking why when I was stuck. You just sat down and helped.

Thanks becomes thank you for remembering the things I never said out loud.

Same sentence. But now it only fits one person. You can't paste it onto anyone else.

We skip the second half because it feels like too much. But it's four extra words. One second of feeling awkward, and they carry it for weeks.

Say it and watch their face. There's a beat where they stop. Then they smile. That beat is them realising you noticed.

Because what people need isn't politeness. They need to know the thing they did landed somewhere. That's what because does.

Pick one person today. Say thank you, and say why.
