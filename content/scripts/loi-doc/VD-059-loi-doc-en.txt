What are you waiting to feel confident enough to start?

Speaking up in meetings. Learning to swim at thirty. Asking for a raise. Selling the cakes you're good at making.

The voice says: not yet. When I'm more sure of myself. When I'm ready.

A year goes by. You're not more ready. You're just a year older, still waiting.

Think back to learning to ride a motorbike. Were you confident the first time? Nobody is.

You rode anyway. Ten times. Twenty times. And one day you noticed you weren't scared anymore.

Nobody waited to stop being scared before getting on. You got on, someone held the back of the seat, and you went.

Confidence doesn't come before you start. It comes after you've done it a few times.

Some things really do need preparing. You don't drive on the highway after one lesson.

But preparing is learning something. Waiting is just hoping a feeling will turn up.

You'll think: what if I'm bad at it?

You probably will be, the first time. Everyone is. That's not a sign to stop. That's just the first time.

Pick the thing you've been waiting on. Do the smallest version of it this week.

Not ready yet? Start anyway. Ready comes later.
