Every group has one person who checks on everyone. When did anyone last check on them?

The one who remembers every birthday. Who books the ride home for whoever had too much. Who sorts out the weddings, the house moves, the last-minute plans.

Everyone loves that person. And everyone assumes they're fine.

Because they never complain. Ask, and they say they're fine, then turn it straight back to you.

People who are used to looking after others often don't know how to ask for themselves. So they wait to be asked.

The one who looks after everyone is usually the last one asked. Not because they're fine.

Some of them really are fine. Looking after people is what they enjoy, with nothing hiding underneath.

So ask once, don't dig. If they say they really are fine, believe them. Knowing someone noticed is enough.

You'll think: someone that strong doesn't need me asking.

Steady doesn't mean never tired. It just means tired where nobody sees.

Whoever gets that question might go quiet for a moment. It's been a long time since anyone asked.

Tonight, think of the person in your group who looks after everyone.

Send them one line: you're always looking after us. How are you doing?
