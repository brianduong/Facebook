Did someone tell you last week about something they had coming up? How did it go?

Your cousin had an interview. A colleague was taking her mum for a check-up. A friend was finally going to ask for a raise.

That day, everyone said something. Good luck. I'm sure it'll be fine.

After that, almost nobody asks again. You included. Not because you don't care. You just forgot.

But they're still carrying it. Got the job or didn't. What the doctor said. What the boss said. And nobody to tell.

Everyone says good luck before. Asking after is how they know you really listened.

Not everything is easy to ask about. Sometimes it didn't go well, and they'd rather not bring it up.

So ask in a way they can step around. How did it go — tell me if you feel like it. If they say it was fine, leave it there.

You'll think: it's been too long, it'd be weird to ask now.

It isn't. A week late still beats nobody asking at all.

Whoever gets that message thinks: so someone really was listening that day.

Tonight, think of one person who told you about something they had coming up.

Send them one line: you mentioned that thing the other day — how did it go?
