## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
How many people served you today whose face you never saw? The one who made your coffee. The one waiting downstairs with your food. The one who cleared your table after you left. You said thank you — you meant it, too. With your eyes on your phone.

From where they're standing, that's a few hundred people a day, and to nearly all of them they're a hand that passes something over. They're used to it. That's the part worth sitting with: nobody looks at you all day, and you get used to it.

This one doesn't cost money. It doesn't cost time either. It costs attention — and attention is what you've been handing out everywhere else. You don't need a clever line. Nothing to think up beforehand. Just look up. Find their face. Say thank you while you're looking at it.

Two seconds. And in those two seconds, somebody stops being a hand and goes back to being a person.

This isn't about making conversation. You don't have to ask how their day is going. If you're shy, that's fine — shy people can look up too. And some days you're the one on the other side. Serving, waiting, standing there. So you know exactly what it feels like when somebody actually looks.

If you're the one behind the counter — tell me about a day somebody looked at you. I'd like to read those.

Small things, said quietly. One video a day.
```

## Nhắc khi đăng

- **CTA đổi phía** — xin người **từng đứng bên kia quầy** kể chuyện của họ. Bình luận dạng
  này thường dài và thật; trả lời từng người, chuyện nào hay thì ghim lên đầu.
- **Dán nguyên khối, đừng tự ngắt dòng giữa câu** — YouTube giữ nguyên chỗ xuống dòng.
- **Đối tượng:** chọn "Không, video này không dành cho trẻ em" — bắt buộc trả lời.
- **Danh mục: Con người và Blog (People & Blogs).** Nằm cuối trang Chi tiết, phải bấm
  "Hiện thêm" mới hiện ra.
- **Dòng `Photos: Pexels.com`** phải giữ — điều khoản API Pexels bắt buộc.
- **Không tự thêm nhạc của YouTube.** Video đã có nhạc nền gốc rồi.
- ⚠️ **Đảo VD-012 với VD-013 khi xếp lịch** — bài này và VD-011 cùng trụ 2.
