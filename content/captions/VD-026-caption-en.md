## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
There's something you're still going with, only because you've already gone too far to stop.

The book you gave up on around page eighty. It's still sitting there. You still won't drop it. The plan you agreed to last week — the day comes and you don't want to go at all, but cancelling feels worse.

And the reason is always the same three words: I've come too far. Eighty pages of tiredness. A week of feeling obliged.

Those eighty pages are gone either way.

Nothing you spend from here buys any of it back. Not one page of it. So the question isn't how much you've put in. It's what the next stretch actually gives you.

This isn't permission to quit the moment it stops being fun. Some things you push through because that's the deal. And some things only get good after the boring bit.

It's just one honest question. Am I still in this because I want it — or because I've already started?

With small things it's easy. Shut the book. Send one apologetic text.

The big ones aren't for a one-minute video. But the course you picked at eighteen, you picked with an eighteen-year-old's head. That person had never met the you sitting here now. Thinking it over once doesn't betray them.

Is there something you're still carrying only because you've carried it this long? Name it in the comments. Naming it takes some of the weight off.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Don't let it read as "quit your job, drop out".** If comments go there, pull back to the
  two guardrails in the script: **some things you push through because you promised**, and
  **the big ones aren't for a one-minute video** — this only asks for one rethink.
- **Never advise anyone to quit a job, a course or a marriage in the replies**, even when
  they tell a real story and ask straight out. Answer at the video's own scale: the book,
  the plan.
- **Avoid "let go", "healing", "be your true self"** in copy, tags and replies.
- **The CTA deliberately avoids "tell me about a time you…"** — VD-022 (22 Aug) used that
  exact frame and is also a pillar-4 episode. This one only asks people to **name** the thing.
  Keep replies at that scale too: naming is enough, don't push for "and then what".
- **Comments will come fast** — naming something is easy to type. Reopen inside 24 hours.
- The previous episode (VD-025) asks for an action; this one asks for one line.
- Select "No, it's not made for kids" and **People & Blogs**.
- Keep `Photos: Pexels.com`; don't add another music track.
