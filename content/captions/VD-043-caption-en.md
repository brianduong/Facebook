## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Some days you're tired before you've done a thing. Count how many undecided things are hanging in your head.

Should you change jobs. Are you going home this weekend. Do you take that shirt back. Do you reply to that person, or let it go. None of them is urgent today. But every time you pass one, you pull it out and think it through again — then put it back for tomorrow.

An undecided thing doesn't sit still. Every day, it makes you think it through from the start.

Some things really can't be decided yet. For those, decide something smaller: the day you'll decide. Write that date down. Until then, you're allowed to stop thinking about it.

Most of these don't have a right answer hiding somewhere. What wears you down isn't picking the wrong one. It's three weeks of picking neither.

Tonight, write down everything that's hanging. Pick the smallest and decide it.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```
