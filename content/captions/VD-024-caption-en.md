## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Let me know if you need anything. You meant that. But nobody ever lets you know, do they?

Think back to your own worst stretch. Somebody sent you that exact line. Did you answer it? No. You didn't either.

Because when you're that far down, working out what you need is its own job. And then you'd have to go and ask for it. That line sounds warm — but it hands the hardest part to the person who has the least left.

People who are drowning don't ask.

So stop asking. Pick one thing and do it. A friend just lost their job? Don't text "let me know". Turn up with food. Somebody's just had a baby? Don't ask what they need — bring a hot meal, do the washing-up, go home.

This isn't a licence to barge into anyone's house. It's just a change of sentence. Instead of "tell me when you're free", try "I'll come Thursday, is that alright?" A sentence with a day in it. Now they only have to nod or shake their head.

Small is fine. A small real thing beats a big open offer.

And they won't remember what you brought. They'll remember that somebody came.

Think of one person in a hard stretch right now. Don't ask what they need — pick one small thing and do it.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Don't let it read as "turn up uninvited".** If a comment goes there, pull it back to the
  boundary the video draws: **you still ask** — you just ask with a day in the sentence, so
  all they have to do is nod or shake their head.
- **Don't call anyone thoughtless in the replies.** Blocks 2 and 3 say it outright: you went
  quiet too. The video stands next to the viewer, not above them.
- **Avoid "journey", "healing", "connection"** in copy, tags and replies.
- **Expect "I did show up and they didn't want to see me".** Answer with the video's own
  line: a sentence with a day in it is also **easier to say no to** — that still beats silence.
- **The CTA is an action for someone else**, with no story asked for, so comments will be few
  and slow. Reopen the post after 3–4 days, like VD-009 and VD-020.
- Select "No, it's not made for kids" and **People & Blogs**.
