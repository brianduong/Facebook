## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
That thing you've been avoiding all week? The whole task isn't hard. The first ten minutes are.

The file you've opened and closed three times. The dishes still in the sink. The message you read yesterday and still haven't answered. Before you start, your mind makes each one feel enormous.

Don't promise to finish. Set a timer for ten minutes. Open the file and write one bad first line. Wash the first bowl. Type the first honest sentence. These ten minutes aren't for doing it well. They're for making the task real instead of letting it grow inside your head.

When the timer rings, you are allowed to stop. Really. That permission matters. If “ten minutes” secretly means “until it's finished,” you won't trust the timer next time.

Sometimes your hands keep moving. Fine. And if you stop, those ten minutes still count. There is less left than before, and now you know where to begin when you come back.

Set ten minutes now. When the timer rings, come back and tell me: stopped, or kept going?

Small things, said quietly. One video a day.
```
