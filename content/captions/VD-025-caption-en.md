## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Can't run your five k? Walk one lap. Can't cook a proper dinner? Instant noodles — drop an egg in.

Not the days you've got energy. I mean the day you open your eyes and there's already nothing in the tank.

On that day most of us do something odd. We write off the whole day. Can't do the proper workout, so no workout. Can't clean the whole room, so nothing gets cleaned. Half-measures feel worse than nothing, we think. So it's all or it's off.

Every job has a smaller version.

Can't do five k — walk one lap round the block. Can't do the whole room — do the corner of the desk. Can't cook properly — instant noodles, and drop an egg in. That's dinner.

This isn't about doing it badly to get it over with. One lap is still a whole lap. And it's one day. Today the tank's empty so you shrink it. Tomorrow you're back, you go back.

The win isn't the lap. The win is that today didn't get thrown away.

Scrap a whole day and the next one is several times harder to start. That thread is slow to build and quick to snap.

And at the end of it the sentence in your head is different. Today I did a little. Not: today I did nothing.

What are you about to drop today because you can't do it properly? Shrink it one notch and do the small version. Just for today.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Don't let it read as "lower the bar forever".** Two guardrails are in the script and both
  belong in the replies: **one lap is still a whole lap**, and **it's one day**.
- **Don't say "the bad version"** in replies — that's VD-018 (*the bad version is the only
  real one*), which is about **quality**. This one is about **size**. Mixing them breaks both.
- **Avoid "discipline", "beat yourself", "nothing is impossible"** — this isn't a productivity
  video.
- **Expect "I shrank it one day and then shrank it for a month".** Answer honestly: that risk
  is real, which is why the video says **just for today**. Don't wave it away.
- **The CTA is a same-day action for yourself**, so comments land in the evening.
- The previous episode (VD-024) is an action **for someone else**; this one is **for you**.
- Select "No, it's not made for kids" and **People & Blogs**.
