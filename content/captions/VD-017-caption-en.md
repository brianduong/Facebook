It's not just the lift. It's the queue. The red light. The two minutes while the water boils. So when was the last time you just sat there, doing nothing at all?

Every gap gets filled, and you're still tired at the end of the day. Because scrolling isn't rest — your head keeps working the whole time, it just gets handed different things to chew on.
