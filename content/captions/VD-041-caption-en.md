## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
What did you finish today? Go on — name them, right now.

Bit of a pause there. But the things you didn't do — those you can list without missing one.

Everyone runs a list in their head. Unfinished things stay on it. Finished things get wiped the second they're done. So at the end of the day you open that list to see how you did, and it shows you one column: what's missing.

Your list only keeps one column. And that's the one you use to mark your own day.

I'm not saying you did loads today. Some days you don't. Some days go past and nothing lands — that happens.

And the wiping isn't a fault. It's the job: remember everything you've finished and there's no room left for what's coming. The broken bit isn't the list. It's marking a whole day with a list that's missing a column.

Today you answered fourteen messages. Cooked something. Quietly fixed the thing that broke yesterday, so nobody ever knew it broke. None of that is on the list. What is on the list is the email you didn't send — one line, sitting there since two in the afternoon.

You're not writing the small things down to feel good. You're writing them down so the list has both columns. Mark anyone with the missing column and they come out useless. And someone who thinks they're useless doesn't work harder tomorrow. They just stop wanting to open the list.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```
