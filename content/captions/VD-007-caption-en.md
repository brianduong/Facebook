Some people stay kind even to the most difficult person in the room. They weren't born like that. Somebody got there first — somebody was patient with them, right at the moment they were hardest to be patient with.

A kid who argued with everyone, and one teacher who didn't send him out of the room. Someone new at work who broke something expensive, and a manager who pulled up a chair and fixed it with them. Someone whose life was coming apart, and a friend who didn't ask why — just sat there until it got late. None of those people changed the world. They changed one person, on one ordinary day. And that person walks out and is patient with somebody else, years later, without even remembering where they got it.

So kindness isn't a personality you're handed at birth. It gets passed to you, and you pass it on. Which means if someone is being difficult with you today, maybe nobody has done that for them yet. This isn't about putting up with anyone — if it's too much, you can walk away from it. It's just that you get to be the first one who doesn't answer sharp with sharp.

## Nhắc khi đăng

- **Dán nguyên khối, đừng tự ngắt dòng giữa câu** — YouTube giữ nguyên chỗ xuống dòng,
  ngắt giữa câu thì trên điện thoại nhìn lởm chởm.
- **Đối tượng:** chọn "Không, video này không dành cho trẻ em" — bắt buộc trả lời, chọn
  sai là tắt mất bình luận.
- **Danh mục: Con người và Blog (People & Blogs).** Nằm cuối trang Chi tiết, phải bấm
  "Hiện thêm" mới hiện ra. Mọi video của cả hai kênh dùng chung danh mục này.
- **Dòng `Photos: Pexels.com`** phải giữ — điều khoản API Pexels bắt buộc ghi nguồn.
- **Không tự thêm nhạc của YouTube.** Video đã có nhạc nền gốc `nen-am-ap` rồi.
- Đăng xong, tự bình luận từ tài khoản kênh **một cái tên có thật** rồi ghim lên đầu —
  CTA kể tên chỉ chạy khi có người mở hàng trước.
