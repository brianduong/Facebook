## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Someone told you you did well today. How long did that stay with you?

Now think of something someone said about your work three years ago. That one you can still quote.

Same kind of person, same kind of sentence. One goes in the ledger. One you wave off.

And you have the reason ready. They were being polite. They say that to everyone. They didn't see the messy part.

You're using two different scales, and only one of them is allowed to move.

Here's the tell. You never do that to criticism. Nobody thinks: they were in a bad mood, they didn't see the good part.

Some praise really is filler. Good job, nice one, thrown over a shoulder on the way past. Fine — let that go. But that's not the one you're throwing away. You throw away the specific ones too.

Someone tells you which part they used, and how. That took attention. That's evidence. And evidence gets filed. You don't get to file only the evidence against.

And yes — you're worried that if you take it in, you'll go soft. You'll stop pushing. It doesn't work like that. People who can hear what's working know where to aim. Everyone else just aims everywhere.

The move is small. Write it down word for word. Not your summary of it. Theirs. Because by the time you summarise it, your version has already shrunk it.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- ⚠️ **Banned words: "confidence", "self-love", "self-worth", "imposter syndrome"** — caption,
  tags and comment replies. They drag it into self-help register. This piece runs on one
  concrete image — **the scales and the ledger** — keep that image throughout.
- ⚠️ **Keep the concession.** Some praise really is filler, and letting that go is fine. Drop it
  and the video asks viewers to swallow every polite remark; the comments will catch it.
- ⚠️ **Keep the "going soft" pair.** Naming the fear without answering it leaves viewers hanging.
  If someone says taking praise makes people complacent, answer with the line already in the
  piece: people who can hear what's working know where to aim.
- ⚠️ **This is NOT part of the "go easy on yourself" cluster** (VD-003 · 013 · 018 · 025 · 029).
  It doesn't ask for a lower bar — it asks you to **keep the books straight**, which is more
  work, not less. Don't reply in a "stop being so hard on yourself" voice.
