Today, did you eat lunch sitting down, or standing up?

On a heavy day, lunch is the first thing to go. A bread roll by the window. Noodles at your desk. Leftover rice at the kitchen counter. You'll eat properly when things calm down — but they don't calm down on their own.

A proper meal won't fix your day. But it keeps you strong enough to fix it.

Fifteen minutes. Sit down. Use a plate. If you don't have fifteen, take five and chew slowly. That still counts.

Tomorrow, find one meal. Sit down for it.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
