## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
There's something you still haven't put down, and part of you thinks putting it down means they win.

Here's how you know it's still there. There's a name you scroll past a little faster than the rest. And there's a conversation you've replayed in your head a hundred times — every time, you come up with a better answer than the last. You're never going to get to use it. They're asleep. They've been sleeping fine for years.

So let's be clear about what forgiving is not. It's not saying it was fine. It wasn't fine. It hurt you, and that stays true. It's not going back to them either — some people you forgive and still never see again, and that's allowed. And it isn't a gift you hand them. They may never even know you did it.

Forgiving is just this: you stop carrying it around every day. The heavy thing all these years wasn't them. It was the carrying.

And no, it doesn't feel light the moment you set it down. Some days you'll pick it back up without noticing. It just gets a little smaller each time you notice you're carrying it, and set it down again.

You don't have to do this today. Today you only have to know that you're allowed to.

So write one line down, somewhere only you will see it: "I've carried this long enough."

Small things, said quietly. One video a day.
```

## Nhắc khi đăng

- ⚠️ **Bài nặng nhất của kênh.** Sẽ có người kể chuyện thật trong bình luận. Trả lời từng
  người bằng một câu công nhận, **đừng khuyên thêm** — video đã nói hết. Và **không hứa
  nhẹ lòng**: cả bài dựng lên để tránh đúng chuyện đó.
- **Dán nguyên khối, đừng tự ngắt dòng giữa câu** — YouTube giữ nguyên chỗ xuống dòng.
- **Đối tượng:** chọn "Không, video này không dành cho trẻ em" — bắt buộc trả lời.
- **Danh mục: Con người và Blog (People & Blogs).** Nằm cuối trang Chi tiết, phải bấm
  "Hiện thêm" mới hiện ra.
- **Dòng `Photos: Pexels.com`** phải giữ — điều khoản API Pexels bắt buộc.
- **Không tự thêm nhạc của YouTube.** Video đã có nhạc nền gốc rồi.
- **Đừng đăng liền sau VD-009** — hai bài đều nói chuyện bên trong, xen một bài trụ 2 vào giữa.
