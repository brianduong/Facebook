## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Someone just asked you for a favor, and you said yes before you even thought about it. You didn't want to. You already knew there was no room for it. Your mouth said yes anyway — because no takes one second longer than yes does, and that one second is where you're afraid of what they'll think of you.

So you take it home. You do it at eleven at night, worn out, just to get it off your back. And afterwards there's nobody to be angry at. Only yourself. That's the part that stays.

But between yes and no there's a third answer, and almost nobody uses it:

"Let me think about it. I'll get back to you tonight."

That's it. Two lines. It isn't a no — nobody has ever been offended by it. What it buys you is the one thing you didn't have: a few hours where fear isn't answering for you. By evening you'll know, and either way it'll be a real answer instead of a reflex.

If it's a no, keep it short. "I can't take this one on. Sorry." Skip the long reason — a long reason is usually just you trying to feel less guilty. Saying no isn't about keeping people out. It's about having something left to give on the day it really matters.

Copy that sentence somewhere you'll find it. Next time someone asks, try it — then come back and tell me how it went.

Small things, said quietly. One video a day.
```

  — đừng gộp vào đoạn trên, người xem copy dòng đó ra.

- **Đối tượng:** chọn "Không, video này không dành cho trẻ em" — bắt buộc trả lời.

  "Hiện thêm" mới hiện ra.

- **Không tự thêm nhạc của YouTube.** Video đã có nhạc nền gốc rồi.
