Have you ever stayed away from someone's sad news because you didn't know what to say?

A colleague's father passes away. You think about going, then think: we're not that close, what would I say? So you don't. But people who've lost someone don't remember what anyone said. They remember who came.

Someone who's grieving doesn't need the right words. They need to see who showed up.

If you can't go, send one short message, just to them: I heard. I'm thinking of you. And a few weeks later, ask again.

Next time you don't know what to say, just come. Being there is enough.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
