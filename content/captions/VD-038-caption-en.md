## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
They answered with one word. And you've already worked out what it means.

Ok. Full stop. That's the whole message.

By the evening you've got the whole story. They're annoyed. Probably about last week. Probably been building for a while.

Nobody told you any of that. You wrote it.

The thing you're most sure they're thinking is usually the thing you're most afraid of.

It isn't only messages. A colleague walks past without saying hi. A friend takes two days. Same machine starts up.

And sometimes you're right. People do get annoyed and not say so. That happens. The problem isn't that you're always wrong. It's that you never check.

So you act on the version in your head. You go cold first, to get there before they do. And now there really is something wrong between you. You built it.

Checking costs one sentence. That felt short — is everything alright?

It feels needy. It feels like making something out of nothing. Compared with three days of running a trial where the other person isn't even in the room.

Most of the time the answer is dull. Phone died. Stuck in a meeting. Nothing to do with you.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```
