## Mô tả

> ⚠️ **Dán nguyên khối, đừng tự ngắt dòng giữa câu.** Mỗi đoạn phải là **một dòng liền**
> — YouTube giữ nguyên chỗ xuống dòng, ngắt giữa câu thì trên điện thoại nhìn lởm chởm.

```
It's raining. Someone holds their umbrella over a stranger at a bus stop. Ten minutes. The bus comes. Neither one asks the other's name.

The stranger was on the way to a job interview. They walked in dry. They got the job. The person with the umbrella never found out.

Kindness doesn't work like a payment you sit and count. It moves — through one person, then another, into places you'll never see.

Small things, said quietly. One video a day.
```
