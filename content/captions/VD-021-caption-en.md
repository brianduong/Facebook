## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Have you noticed? We're kindest on the days we've got time.

On an easy day it's nothing. Hold the door, hear the whole story, let the other car go first. On a day you're running late — same person, same thing — and you swat it away.

The lift doors are closing and someone comes running. Your hand knows what to do. But you're already fifteen minutes late for a meeting. A colleague turns to tell you something right as you shut the laptop, and you go mm, yeah, mm, until they stop.

And afterwards you explain it to yourself: I was genuinely busy today. Which is true, by the way.

Being kind when you're free is easy. Anyone can do that.

It's the inconvenient day that tells you whether it's actually yours.

This isn't asking you to be available to everybody all the time. Nobody can carry that. Look at what it actually costs: holding a door, five seconds. Hearing the end of a story, two minutes. What stops you isn't those five seconds — it's the feeling of being slowed down.

And the other person can't see your day. They get one thing out of it: somebody held the door.

And some day it's you running for that lift. On your worst day of the month.

Odd thing, though: the kind ones you did in a rush are the ones you still remember years later.

Today, once, be kind at the least convenient moment you get. Then tell me how hard it was.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Don't let it read as "be available to everyone, always".** If someone comments that
  they're too burnt out to be kind, answer inside the boundary the video draws: **five
  seconds, two minutes**, once in a day — not a sacrifice.
- **Don't call anyone thoughtless in the replies.** Block 6 says it plainly: busy is really
  busy. The video stands next to the person in a rush, not above them.
- **Avoid "spread the love", "positive energy", "what you give comes back"** in copy, tags
  and replies.
- **The CTA is a same-day action plus a story**, so comments land in the evening. Reopen
  the post at the end of the day.
- Different from VD-002 · VD-011 · VD-012 · VD-020 (same pillar): this one takes the
  **timing** angle — same kind act, done free or done rushed.
- Select "No, it's not made for kids" and **People & Blogs**.
- Keep `Photos: Pexels.com`; don't add another music track.
