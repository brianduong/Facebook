## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Eight hours of sleep. You wake up just as heavy. So where is that coming from?

Same at the weekend. Two days of lying around and Sunday night you feel worse, not better.

None of this means sleep doesn't matter. Going to bed on time is still the job. This is about what's left over after you've already slept enough.

Here's the strange bit. That heaviness lifts sometimes, and not because you rested. You sit down with something you actually wanted to do, and twenty minutes in, it's gone.

Some tiredness isn't about hours. It's about how much of the day was yours.

A day where every hour belonged to somebody else's list is heavy even if you slept nine hours the night before. And a short night before a day you chose can end up lighter than a long one before a day you didn't.

This isn't a hint to change your whole life. Most of the day isn't up to you, and that isn't going to change tonight. One corner is enough to test it: fifteen minutes with your name on it.

It sounds backwards — you're tired, so you should rest, not do something. Try it once anyway. If it doesn't work you've lost fifteen minutes.

One thing though. If it's been heavy for months, that's a doctor's question, not a video's.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Never use "burnout", "exhaustion", "healing", "live for yourself"** in copy, tags or
  replies. **"Burnout" especially** — it drags the video into medical territory, which the
  closing line deliberately steps around.
- ⚠️ **Stay out of medical territory.** No illness, depression, anaemia, iron levels — even
  if someone asks directly in the comments. The open door is already in the script and
  **must stay exactly that short**: months of it is a doctor's question. Any longer and it
  reads as a scare.
- ⚠️ **This does not contradict VD-008** (*the hour you steal back at midnight*, 31 Jul).
  Sleep still matters — the guard rail sits early in the script; keep it in replies. This
  one is only about **what's left after you've slept enough**.
- ⚠️ **This is not a "go easy on yourself" episode** (VD-003 · 013 · 018 · 025 · 029). It
  **doesn't ask anyone to lower the bar** — it points at a different cause. Don't reply in
  a "give yourself permission to rest" voice.
- ⚠️ **Don't let it become "quit and go find your passion".** The guard rail is there: most
  of the day isn't up to you and tonight won't change it — the ask is **one fifteen-minute
  corner**.
- **This is not VD-029** (*rest isn't the reward*, 7 Sep): that one removes the **conditions**
  people put in front of rest; this one says rest **doesn't fix** this kind of tiredness.
  Five slots apart, and deliberately not cross-referenced — don't pull them together in
  replies either.
- Select "No, it's not made for kids" and **People & Blogs**.
- Keep `Photos: Pexels.com`; don't add another music track.
