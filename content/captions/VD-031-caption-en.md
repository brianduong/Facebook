## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
That trolley you pushed back to the rack. Who did it help? You'll never see their face.

Same with the chair you slid back under the table on your way out. The kettle you filled up again before you left the room. Nobody watched you do it. Nobody will ever come and thank you for it. The person it helps hasn't arrived yet — they'll walk in twenty minutes from now and never know anything happened.

Some kindness isn't for anyone you'll meet. It's for whoever comes next.

This isn't about people who don't do it. Plenty of days you don't either, and that's fine. It's just that this kind is strange, and worth noticing. There's nothing in it for you at all. No thank you. No one nodding at you. Not even the small warm bit where someone sees you being decent.

Which is exactly why it counts. There was no audience to do it for.

And it goes the other way round too. Someone left a clean table for you this week. You didn't notice, because nothing was wrong. That's the whole thing about it — when it works, nobody counts it.

It costs almost nothing. Three seconds. One hand.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Never use "civic-minded", "good citizens", "be a decent human"** in copy, tags or replies.
  That flips this into a lecture about public behaviour, which is exactly the failure mode
  this idea was flagged for. Avoid "spread kindness" too.
- **Don't let the comments become a list of complaints** about people who leave a mess. If it
  starts, pull back to the line the script already put there: **plenty of days you don't
  either, and that's fine**.
- **This is not VD-011** (*a good deed nobody knows about*, 5 Aug): there the **doer** goes
  unseen. Here the **recipient** hasn't arrived. Say that plainly if someone brings it up.
- **It's also not VD-002** (*kindness is never wasted*), which follows a good deed through
  several people. This one stops at one anonymous person.
- **The CTA asks for a specific place**, so expect good concrete examples in the comments.
  Write them down — the idea bank is empty from VD-037 onward.
