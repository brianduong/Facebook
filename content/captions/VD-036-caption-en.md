## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
You can be kind to almost anyone. Except the person who just got something wrong for you.

The new one at the busy place brings the wrong thing, hands shaking. The junior sends the same mistake back a second time. The reply lands nowhere near the question you asked. And something arrives in your chest before you've decided anything — that's not a character flaw, that's just how fast it is.

And the mistake still has to be said out loud. Somebody has to fix it. That part isn't in question.

The kindness isn't in swallowing it. It's in dropping the one extra sentence you added for yourself.

You know the one. It isn't about fixing anything — it's the one that lands after the fixing is done. "Every single time." "Did you read it at all?" That sentence costs more than it looks. The work gets fixed either way. The person just stops telling you things, and the next mistake gets hidden a bit longer, because telling you is the expensive part now.

This isn't asking you to go soft. Say the thing. Say it clearly. Say it today. Just take out one sentence — the one that isn't for the work.

This is harder than being kind when you have time. That one costs you nothing. Here you're paying for it: your afternoon just got longer because of what they did. Which is exactly why it counts for anything at all.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Never use "emotional control", "self-mastery", "be the bigger person", "forgiveness"**
  in copy, tags or replies. Those pull it into moralising; this one is practical.
- ⚠️ **This does not ask anyone to swallow it.** Both guard rails stay in replies: **the
  mistake still has to be said**, and **say it, say it clearly, say it today**. One sentence
  comes out — the one that isn't for the work.
- ⚠️ **Don't blame the viewer for being annoyed.** It arrives before you decide anything;
  the script says so outright. If someone reads it as "so I'm a bad person", pull back to
  that line.
- **The actual argument, don't garble it:** the extra sentence **doesn't damage the work** —
  the work gets fixed anyway. It damages **the flow of information back to you**: next time
  the mistake gets hidden longer.
- **This is not VD-021** (*anyone can be kind when they're free*, 26 Aug): there the cost is
  being **busy**; here it's being **annoyed**, at the person who caused it. Not VD-012
  either — that one is seeing people when nothing has gone wrong.
- ⚠️ **VD-031 (5 Sep) is also a Small-kindness episode** — five slots apart on purpose.
- Select "No, it's not made for kids" and **People & Blogs**.
- Keep `Photos: Pexels.com`; don't add another music track.
