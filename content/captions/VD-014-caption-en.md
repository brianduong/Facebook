## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Your bike didn't break down today. Nobody at home got sick. Did you count either of those?

At the end of the day, you count what went wrong. The reply you forgot. The thing you left unfinished. The bill that came out higher than you thought. Broken things make noise, so you hear them. Working things stay quiet. The tap ran this morning. The power stayed on all day.

You only ever notice them on the day they fail. The morning with no water. The blackout in the middle of your work.

And some good things are only good because they didn't happen. The toothache that stayed away. The phone call you were dreading that never came. There is nowhere to write those down, so a day where nothing went wrong looks like a blank page.

It wasn't empty. You just counted the broken things only. All day you stood on things that held: the bike started, the road home was clear, the person you called picked up.

This isn't about cheering up. A hard day is still hard, and nothing here makes it lighter. It's just counting properly. The broken things already got counted. The ones that held should get counted too.

Tell me one thing that didn't break today. The smaller the better.
```
