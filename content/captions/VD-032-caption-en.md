## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
The most embarrassing thing you still remember — how old is it now? Three years? Ten?

And it still plays. In full. Right down to what you were wearing. The hello in the corridor that landed on nobody. The sentence you tripped over in a meeting. The message that went into the wrong window.

Now try this. What was the person next to you wearing yesterday? You can't remember. It was yesterday, and they were right there.

You keep the full cut. Everyone else keeps the trailer, and loses it by Friday.

This isn't the sad version where nobody cares about you. People care. They just have their own reel running — their own corridor, their own tripped-up sentence from their own ten years ago. You remember yours in that much detail for one reason: you were the only person in the room who felt it.

None of this means just get over it. You can't decide to stop remembering something. Only one thing changes: next time it plays, you know you're watching it alone. And a thing you're watching alone is smaller than a thing you think a roomful of people is still watching with you.

Ten years is a long time to keep screening something for an audience of one.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Never use "confidence", "overcome your fear", "self-love", "healing"** in copy, tags or
  replies.
- ⚠️ **Never let this collapse into "nobody cares about you".** It sounds brutal and it says
  *you don't matter*, which is the opposite of the point. The right line is already in the
  script: **people do care, they just have their own reel running**.
- ⚠️ **The CTA asks for a number, not a story.** When replying to comments, **don't ask what
  happened** — the whole subject is something people are embarrassed about.
- **This is not VD-015** (*talk to yourself like a friend*, 10 Aug): that one fixes the
  **tone** of the inner voice. This one fixes the **size of the audience** you imagine.
- **It's also not VD-030** (*you post the edit too*, scheduled 21 Sep): that one looks at
  **other people online**, this one looks at **your own memory**. Five slots apart on purpose.
