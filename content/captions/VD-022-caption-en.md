## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
Not settled by twenty-eight. Not married by thirty. Sound familiar? Hang on though — who set that deadline?

You've heard it at a family dinner. Someone turns to you: at your age, so-and-so's kid already had a house. You laugh it off at the table. But it doesn't stay at the table. It follows you home.

And after a while nobody has to say it any more. You measure yourself: by thirty I should have this, by thirty-five that.

So ask it once, out loud: that timetable — who actually wrote it? Nobody signed it. There's no document anywhere. It's people passing around what they heard from someone else.

Look at the people you finished school with. One married early, one went back to study, one changed careers at thirty-four. Nobody is on anybody's schedule.

Arriving later than someone else is not the same as not arriving.

This isn't a licence to stop trying and call it going at your own pace. You still do the work. You just put down somebody else's stopwatch.

There are people who start university at thirty. Four years later they walk out with the degree, same as anyone. Those four years were going to pass anyway — the only question was what you'd be holding at the end of them.

The tiring part was never being slow. It's walking while turning your head to check where everyone else has got to.

And your road has stretches nobody sees. The year someone at home was ill. The year it all broke and you sat down and started again. Those don't go on anyone's scoreboard.

Tell me one thing you did later than everyone else — the one you're glad now you did at all.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```

## Posting notes

- **Don't let it read as "stop trying, take it easy".** If a comment goes there, reply
  inside the boundary the video draws: **you still do the work** — the video only puts down
  somebody else's stopwatch.
- **Don't attack the relative who says "at your age…".** Most of the time they're family
  and they're repeating a line they were handed. The video stands with the person hearing
  it, not against the person saying it.
- **Avoid "success", "peak", "best version of yourself"** in replies — that vocabulary drags
  the piece back into the self-help voice it's arguing against.
- **The CTA asks for a story in the comments**, so replies come fast and early. Reopen the
  post inside the first 24 hours.
- Different from VD-006 (*comparing yourself to others*): that one is about **scoreboards**,
  this one is about **deadlines**.
