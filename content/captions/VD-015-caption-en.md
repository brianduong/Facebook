## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
That thing you just said to yourself in your head. Try saying it to your best friend. You wouldn't.

"Only that, and you still messed it up." "Who even asks a question like that." That sentence goes past fast — fast enough that you never catch what you just said to yourself.

Now swap the person. Your closest friend just made the exact same mistake. They message you, already blaming themselves. Would you say "only that, and you still messed it up"? No. You'd say: "It happens. Anyone would have."

Same mistake. Two completely different sentences. The kind one you keep for other people.

You're not being cruel on purpose. You think the harsh sentence is what stops you doing it again. But it doesn't work that way. After the telling-off you're just tired, and slower to try again.

Notice this: your friend isn't lying to you. They can see the mistake too. They just add the half you left out — it went wrong, and you can go again tomorrow.

So next time you catch that sentence, don't argue with it. Arguing keeps you in there all evening. Write it once, the way you'd send it to your friend. It'll feel awkward. Write it anyway.

Catch one today and rewrite it. Leave the rewrite below if you like.

```
