## 2 · Mô tả — copy khối này

```
You were about to rest. Then you thought: I haven't finished, I haven't earned it yet. Who made that rule?

You lie down and your head keeps running the list of what's left. You watch one episode and feel guilty the whole way through. You sit still for ten minutes and it feels like hiding.

Your bike runs low, you fill it up. Nobody asks whether it did enough today first.

Rest isn't the reward. Rest is the petrol.

A reward you have to earn. Petrol you put in when it runs low — nothing else gets asked. And there's a catch inside "rest when the work is done": the work is never done. Finish tonight and tomorrow brings more. So waiting until it's done is waiting for something that doesn't arrive.

This isn't telling you to drop everything and lie down. The work still gets done. Just stop making it finish before you're allowed to sit down. And it doesn't have to be a good rest either — not a book, not a walk, nothing to show for it. Sitting there counts.

The easiest way is to book it in. Nine o'clock. Thirty minutes. Nothing gets to move it.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
```
