When did you last notice what your home sounds like?

The broom on the yard at six. The kettle. Dishes in the sink at night. You don't hear any of it, until you move away and the first night in a new room is too quiet.

The sounds you stop hearing at home are the ones you'll miss the most.

If you live alone, listen to where you are now: the neighbour's cooking, motorbikes at midnight.

Tonight, sit still for one minute. Count how many sounds you can name.

Small things, said quietly. A new one every couple of days.
